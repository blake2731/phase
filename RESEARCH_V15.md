# PHASE v15 Research Note

## Topic
Auditory meaning and narrative transportation.

## Research used

1. Designing informative warning signals: Effects of indicator type, modality, and task demand on recognition speed and accuracy.
   Finding used: signals are easier and faster to recognize when their structure has a meaningful relationship to the event rather than being an arbitrary symbol.

2. Neural signatures of attentional engagement during narratives and its consequences for event memory.
   Finding used: emotional narrative engagement predicts later memory for story events.

3. The Power of the Picture: How Narrative Film Captures Attention and Disrupts Goal Pursuit.
   Finding used: a coherent contiguous narrative captures attention more strongly than the same scenes presented without causal continuity.

4. Stories in Action.
   Principle used: narratives become meaningful through temporal events, goal oriented agents and causal sequences.

## Design translation

PHASE should not use one generic success sound for every positive event. Repeated event families should build recognizable auditory identities.

### v15 sound grammar

Discovery / understood law
Low foundation, fifth, octave resolution.
Meaning: the model settles.

Observation
Higher unresolved rising contour.
Meaning: interesting, but not necessarily solved.

World change
Low onset followed by an expanding upward sonority.
Meaning: the environment itself changed.

Anomaly
Unusual 11/8 and 7/4 relationships without normal octave closure.
Meaning: this does not fit the current model.

Mode acquisition
The contour reflects the learned prime where practical.
Meaning: P gained a reproducible pattern.

Bond
Two sustained voices converge into a shared octave.
Meaning: two systems now act together.

Key item
Five note compact contour with a low structural tone.
Meaning: a concrete structure was acquired.

## Opening story change

The rupture should destroy an experienced normal, not merely an explained normal.

v15 opening causal chain:

Origin exists
P wakes the four familiar lights
The four notes are explicitly framed as P's oldest ritual
P returns to the center
The old song repeats
The song breaks
Three notes scatter

The added FOUR NOTES memory beat is intentionally short. Its job is to establish emotional causality, not provide lore exposition.
