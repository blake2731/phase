PHASE

A small browser game made entirely from procedural mathematics.

HOW TO RUN

1. Open index.html in a modern browser.
2. Click ENTER THE FIELD.
3. Move with WASD or Arrow Keys.
4. Emit a wave with Space or Click.
5. Change your active prime frequency with Q and E.
6. Match your frequency to the number inside an enemy to trigger resonance.

WHAT IS PROCEDURAL

Every visible game object is generated by code.

Enemy bodies use radial trigonometric functions.
Wave weapons are expanding circles with mathematically defined fronts.
Wave intersections are calculated from real circle intersection geometry.
Enemy symmetry is controlled by prime numbers.
The boss cycles through 3, 5, and 7.
Movement trails are generated from recorded player coordinates.
Explosions are radial systems seeded by enemy symmetry.
Audio is generated live with the Web Audio API.

There are no image files, texture files, sprite sheets, music files, or sound files.
