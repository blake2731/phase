# PHASE v16 Tester Reference

Play naturally first. Use this only after your first impression.

## Emotional spine to test

### 1. Build home

Wake the four lights.

Expected feeling: I am participating in a familiar ritual, not merely activating four switches.

### 2. Hear the Answer

Return to Origin and press Space.

Expected sequence:

Origin sends four notes.

A faint fivefold form beyond the eastern edge responds with the final note.

Story moment: AN ANSWER.

Expected feeling: something outside home is connected to it.

### 3. Lose the relation

Continue from AN ANSWER.

Expected:

Origin breaks.

Three notes scatter.

The external answering form disappears and its response goes silent.

The rupture card should mention both the missing home notes and lost response.

### 4. Restore home

Recover all three lost notes.

Expected:

The local Origin chord is restored.

The larger problem is NOT resolved.

Story moment: HOME IS WHOLE.

The four note call plays again, but the external fifth response is missing. A weak broken echo replaces it.

Expected feeling: restoring home mattered, but something beyond home is still wrong.

Current Thread should become: Follow the broken Answer.

### 5. Follow the broken signal

Continue east through the existing progression.

Expected interpretation: the fivefold signal should no longer feel like a random next object. It is carrying the damaged response that once answered Origin.

### 6. Restore Phi

Complete the fivefold garden.

Expected order:

PHI acquisition first.

Then, after that acquisition fully clears, a separate story moment appears.

Story moment: THE ANSWER.

The clean answering note returns.

Expected reveal: Phi was the voice that answered Origin.

Expected continuation: Phi is still listening east, so the larger thread remains unresolved.

## Journal

After Origin is repaired, Bonds should contain THE ANSWER as unresolved.

After Phi is restored, the same bond should update rather than creating a second unrelated lore item.

## Report immediately if

1. The Answer appears before P initiates the old song.
2. The fifth response is not visually distinguishable from ordinary effects.
3. HOME IS WHOLE feels like another popup rather than a bittersweet resolution.
4. The broken fivefold signal still feels unrelated to the lost Answer.
5. The Phi reveal feels too obvious, too sentimental, or too confusing.
6. Any acquisition and story moment overlap.
7. The new thread makes the early game feel too slow.
8. You stop caring about the Answer before reaching Phi.

## Most important subjective question

After repairing Origin, do you personally want to know what happened to the thing that answered it?

If the answer is no, the emotional spine is not doing its job even if every trigger works technically.
