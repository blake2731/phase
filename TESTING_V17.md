# PHASE v17 Tester Notes

Play naturally first.

## Journal

Open the Journal after several observations are collected.

Check that:

1. Observations span the full Journal width.
2. Formulae such as `f_mote ← f_P`, `P ≠ origin`, `2 : 3 : 5`, and `f_out = f_in` remain on one readable badge rather than colliding with the title.
3. Titles are immediately scannable.
4. Diagrams are specific to the observation rather than repeated generic crosshair art.
5. Effects remain readable but visually secondary.

## Early displacement pickup

Collect the early target-like `P ≠ origin` observation.

Expected Journal entry:

Title: DISPLACEMENT

Meaning: P can occupy a position independent of Origin. That distinction turns displacement into a usable property of motion.

The wording should make sense regardless of whether it was collected before or after following the signal.

## Ambient music

Listen during ordinary exploration for at least one full pad cycle.

The recurring long ominous bass drone should be gone. Instead there should be a quieter moving low contour beneath the existing procedural music.

Report whether the new baseline feels more like curiosity and space, and whether the moving bass is too busy, too quiet, or still ominous.
