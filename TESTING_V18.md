# PHASE v18 Playtest

## Journal acquisition

Acquire the Field Journal.

Wait until OPEN JOURNAL becomes available, then press J instead of clicking the button.

Expected: the acquisition closes and the Journal opens. There should be no stuck overlay and no extra movement input.

## Early displacement observation

Pick up the early target-like P ≠ origin observation.

Expected wording:

DISPLACEMENT

P ≠ origin

P can occupy a position independent of Origin. That distinction makes displacement measurable and useful.

The text must not claim you found it before or after following the signal.

## Synthesis

The Synthesis section appears in the Journal once at least one ingredient pattern is known. Recipes with only one known ingredient show the second pattern as unknown.

First recipe:

Survey Lens = P ≠ origin + f_mote ← f_P

Expected: once both observations are known, SYNTHESIZE becomes available. Crafting does not remove either observation. The Survey Lens becomes the one active construct and appears orbiting P.

While equipped, a compatible structure just beyond current Pulse range should receive a faint targeting treatment.

Second recipe:

Resonance Coil = 2 → 4 → 8 + Δt

Expected: every fourth player Pulse gains 28 percent additional reach and receives an extra visual ring and sound.

Third recipe:

Phi Thread = φ + ∮

Expected: with Phi repaired, the thread periodically points from Phi toward a nearby unresolved collectible. It does not collect the item automatically.

Only one construct should be equipped at a time. Crafted constructs can be swapped freely and ingredients are never consumed.

## Boundaries

Travel into the Deep Field and deliberately approach the bottom edge.

Expected: the bottom coherence boundary is visibly bright enough to read before collision. It should brighten somewhat as P approaches.

Test the old southern continuations near x approximately 1900, 2760, 2980, and 3970.

Expected: any remaining collision wall has a visible animated seam. P should never stop against completely empty space.

## Feel

Does Synthesis feel like an RPG system that rewards exploration, or like another menu?

Does equipping one construct create an interesting choice?

Are the boundary effects clear without becoming visually noisy?
