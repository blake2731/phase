# PHASE v17 Research Notes

## Topic
Visual hierarchy in dense interfaces and affective priming by music.

## Research used

### Visual hierarchy
An eye tracking study of dashboard complexity found that richer, more complicated layouts consume limited attentional resources and reduce efficient visual processing. A systematic review of integrated information visualization similarly recommends prioritizing core information, establishing explicit hierarchy, reducing clutter, and removing nonessential visual elements.

PHASE implication: the Journal should not give formula, title, prose, effect, and diagram equal visual weight. Formulae should be compact identifiers, titles should be dominant, prose should be secondary, effects tertiary, and diagrams should exist only when they add information.

### Music and affective priming
Experimental work shows that scary music can bias interpretation of otherwise ambiguous material toward threat. Research on musical affect also emphasizes expectation and resolution as ways to create longing, anticipation, suspense, and seeking.

PHASE implication: routine exploration should not continuously use the same deep sustained anchor that can read as foreboding. The deepest register is more valuable if it is reserved for rupture, anomaly, danger, or unresolved story events.

## Changes derived from the research

Journal

1. Observations span the Journal width and use an internal two column layout.
2. Long mathematical formulae are readable badges, not text forced into 29 pixel square icons.
3. Generic repeated sketches are removed.
4. Only observation specific diagrams are retained.
5. Observation title, formula, explanation, effect, and diagram are visually separated.

Displacement discovery

The old copy said the player checked the edge before following the signal even if that was not true. The discovery is now named DISPLACEMENT with time independent copy: P can occupy a position independent of Origin, and that distinction turns displacement into a usable property of motion.

Ambient score

1. Routine pads no longer sustain the approximately 55 Hz area fundamental for several seconds.
2. Sustained harmony is voiced an octave higher.
3. A quiet four note bass contour moves through consonant ratios instead of sitting on one ominous note.
4. Deep low tones remain available for intentional tension events.
