# PHASE v18 Research Notes

## Topic: crafting as progression, strategy, and feedback

This pass started from Stone Story RPG as a reference point, but not as a template. Its public description emphasizes a simple but deep crafting system in which item combinations enable new strategies. The useful principle for PHASE is combination with consequence, not weapons, loot tiers, or resource grinding.

A broader academic analysis of crafting systems identifies several useful dimensions: recipe definition, action fidelity, completion constraints, variable outcomes, system recognition, player expressiveness, and progression. For PHASE, the most important are system recognition and progression. If the player synthesizes something, the world should behave differently because of it.

The v18 Synthesis system therefore uses retained mathematical observations as patterns. Patterns are not consumed. Compatible discoveries can be bound into one active field construct. This keeps exploration valuable and avoids turning the game into material farming.

Initial constructs:

1. Survey Lens: displacement + copied resonance. Reveals compatible structures slightly beyond current Pulse range.
2. Resonance Coil: octave relation + retained timing. Every fourth Pulse travels farther.
3. Phi Thread: golden ratio + closed path. Phi periodically points toward a nearby unresolved observation.

Only one construct can remain coherent at a time. The goal is strategic reconfiguration rather than a permanent pile of upgrades.

## Topic: game feel

Research surveying game feel describes amplification as feedback that communicates the importance and consequence of player actions. v18 therefore makes constructs visible around P and gives their active effects specific visual or audio feedback instead of leaving them as invisible percentages.

## Boundary clarity

The expanded Deep Field inherited collision seams from older versions, but some renderers stopped visually representing those seams below the original map height. v18 explicitly redraws outer coherence edges and southern continuations of internal barriers. Collision and visual topology should agree.
