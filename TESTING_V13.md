# PHASE v13 Tester Reference

Play normally first. The main question is whether important moments now feel separated from ordinary play.

## Stress test the anti mash behavior

1. At ORIGIN, repeatedly tap Space and Enter during the story reveal.
   Expected: the card should not disappear while you are mashing.
   Stop pressing for a moment, then make one fresh press.
   Expected: it closes normally.

2. Repeat the same test on PRIME PULSE or MODE acquisition.
   Expected: the symbol, title, and meaning arrive in stages. Repeated confirmation presses do not instantly dismiss it.

3. Hold a movement key during a story moment.
   Expected: P remains still. There is a brief recovery beat after closing before movement resumes.

## Major event boundaries

### Origin breaks
Expected sequence: visual pause, title, meaning, deliberate continue.

### Origin restored
Expected: Mode 3 acquisition and Origin restoration do not overlap. Finish the acquisition first. Then ORIGIN HOLDS receives its own moment.

### Fivefold Gate
Expected: when Phi installs the Fivefold Key, one major story moment appears: A PATH EXISTS.
There should not be an immediate second cinematic saying the signal continues east.

## Ordinary information

Resonators, small observations, area names, and routine quest changes should remain lightweight. Report any moment that feels over dramatized.

1. Where did the pacing finally feel dramatic?
