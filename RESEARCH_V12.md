# PHASE v12 Research Pass: Curiosity

## Research idea

This pass uses the information-gap view of curiosity and later work showing that curiosity can improve memory for subsequently learned material.

Key design interpretation:

1. Curiosity is stronger when a player can perceive a specific gap between what they know and what they want to know.
2. Pleasant exploration and the tension of an unresolved question are related but distinct forms of curiosity.
3. Anticipation matters. A meaningful unknown should exist before its answer.

## PHASE translation

The game already contains visible but unreachable structures. v12 stops treating those only as level geometry and starts remembering them as explicit unresolved relationships.

The Journal now records Open Questions when P encounters a meaningful unknown. The question remains unresolved until the player gains the capability or information that answers it.

No extra quest marker is created. The question belongs to the player's model of the world, not the mandatory objective list.

## Questions currently tracked

1. THE DISTANT TRIANGLE
   Gap: Mode 3 is visible but initial Pulse reach is insufficient.
   Answer: increased Pulse range reaches it and reveals Mode 3.

2. ONE STRUCTURE, TWO MODES
   Gap: the distant superposition structure encodes both 2 and 3.
   Answer: both modes reach it and overlap.

3. BEYOND P'S EDGE
   Gap: a fivefold object exists where P cannot stand.
   Answer: Phi can remain coherent beyond the boundary and retrieve it.

4. THE QUIET WELL
   Gap: the Echo Well seems to hold a Pulse.
   Answer: the same mode returns after a delay.

5. THE RELAY IS NOT THE SOURCE
   Gap: the apparent endpoint is only repeating something else.
   Answer: the signal continues to the larger fivefold source.

## Visual rule

Unresolved relationships use an incomplete ring. Resolved relationships briefly close that ring.

A Pulse that terminates just short of a meaningful target causes a faint response from the target. This is intended to communicate `almost` without text.
